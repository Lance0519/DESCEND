### INFORMED CONSENT

**Study title:** DESCEND — Filipino Family Lineage–Based Type 2 Diabetes Risk Awareness Survey  

**Purpose**  
You are invited to take part in an educational research survey about Type 2 diabetes (T2DM) risk awareness. This survey asks about your background, lifestyle, optional blood-lab values (if you know them), and family history of diabetes. Your answers will help researchers study risk-awareness patterns and improve an educational tool called DESCEND.

**What you will do**  
You will answer online questions. This usually takes about **10–20 minutes**. Some questions are optional (for example, fasting glucose and HbA1c if you do not know them).

**Voluntary participation**  
Participation is **voluntary**. You may skip questions where allowed, stop at any time, or refuse to participate without penalty.

**Risks and benefits**  
There is little physical risk. You may feel uncomfortable answering personal or family health questions. This survey **does not diagnose** diabetes and **is not medical advice**. If results or questions worry you, please consult a licensed healthcare professional. There is no guaranteed direct medical benefit to you; your participation may help improve educational research on diabetes awareness.

**Confidentiality and data use**  
Responses will be used for academic/thesis research and educational system improvement. We will treat your information as confidential and will not publish names or other direct identifiers. Data will be stored securely and accessed only by the research team. Please do not include unnecessary personal identifiers in open-text answers.

**Data Privacy (Philippines)**  
This study aims to handle personal information in line with applicable privacy principles under the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**.

**Contact for questions**

## PART I — Information Sheet

### 1. Introduction  
You are being invited to take part in a research survey. Before you decide, please read this information carefully. You may ask questions at any time using the contact email below. Participation is voluntary.

### 2. Purpose of the research  
This study collects survey responses related to Type 2 diabetes risk awareness among participants, including demographic, lifestyle, optional laboratory self-report values, and family history of Type 2 diabetes. The goal is to support academic research and improve an educational awareness tool (DESCEND). The study does **not** provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or clinical care.

### 3. Type of research intervention  
This is an **online questionnaire / survey** (Google Form). No blood draw, physical examination, or clinical procedure will be performed by the research team as part of this form.

### 4. Participant selection  
You may be invited if you are an adult (**18 years or older**) who can understand this consent information and complete an online survey. If you have already been diagnosed with Type 2 diabetes, some questions may still apply for educational/research pathway purposes, but the survey is not a substitute for clinical management.

### 5. Voluntary participation  
You do not have to join this study. If you choose not to participate, or if you stop at any time, there will be no penalty. You may refuse to answer optional items.

### 6. Procedures  
If you agree:
1. You will confirm consent on this form;
2. You will answer questions about yourself, lifestyle, optional blood values (if known), and family history of Type 2 diabetes;
3. Your responses will be recorded in the form response spreadsheet / research dataset for analysis.

Approximate time: **10–20 minutes**.

### 7. Risks  
Possible risks include mild emotional discomfort when answering health or family-history questions, and residual privacy risk common to online data collection. There is no clinical procedure risk from completing this form.

**Important:** Self-reported glucose or HbA1c values, if entered, are for research/education only. Abnormal values should be discussed with a licensed clinician. Do not change medications or treatment based on this survey.

### 8. Benefits  
You may not receive a direct personal medical benefit. Possible benefits include contributing to research on diabetes risk awareness and supporting improvement of educational tools. Any awareness feedback from related systems is educational only.

### 9. Reimbursements  
No payment is provided for completing this survey (unless your local study protocol states otherwise).

### 10. Confidentiality  
The research team will keep your responses confidential to the extent reasonably possible. Identifiable information (if any) will be limited and protected. Results may be presented in academic reports, thesis documents, or publications in aggregate/anonymous form so that individuals cannot be easily identified.

### 11. Sharing of research findings / data  
De-identified or aggregated findings may be shared with academic supervisors, panelists, or in thesis/publication outputs. Raw identifiable data will not be publicly posted.

### 12. Right to refuse or withdraw  
You may refuse to participate or withdraw before submitting. After submission, removal may be limited if responses are anonymous and cannot be linked back to you.
